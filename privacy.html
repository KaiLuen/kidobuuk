KidoBuuk Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated：2026-10-24　·　生效日 / Effective：2026-10-24

簡介

KidoBuuk（以下稱「本 App」）是一款為 2 至 6 歲幼兒設計的有聲故事繪本 App，讓孩子從故事場景中認識生活裡的事物，並透過「看圖、選聲音」的小測驗複習。本 App 由 CityFun（以下稱「我們」）開發與維運。本政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資料、為什麼要處理、以及資料會傳送到哪裡。

重點摘要：本 App 不需要註冊、不需要帳號，也不會蒐集姓名、生日、電話、電子郵件或任何可以辨識身分的個人資料。

本 App 不使用麥克風、相機或位置，沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，也沒有任何付費購買項目。

本 App 只會從 Google Firebase 下載故事內容，不會上傳任何關於您或孩子的資料。您的設定只存在自己的裝置上。

一、我們處理的資料

1. 下載故事內容

  • 本 App 會從 Google Firebase 的 Cloud Storage 下載故事清單與封面、故事的圖片與音檔，以及一個記錄最新 App 版本的小檔案。
  • 這些都只是下載。下載時不會附帶帳號、裝置識別碼或任何可辨識您或孩子身分的資訊。

2. 儲存在裝置上的資料（不會離開裝置）

以下資料保存在本 App 於裝置上的私有儲存區，僅供本 App 讀取，不會上傳到任何地方：

項目內容目的
語言設定繁體中文或英文（第一次開啟時依系統語言自動選擇）記住介面與故事的語言
音量設定背景音樂音量、互動音效音量記住使用者調整過的音量
隱私權同意紀錄同意的政策版本與日期判斷是否需要再次顯示本政策
更新提示紀錄上次提示更新時的 App 版本避免重複跳出同一則更新提示
已下載的內容故事清單、封面、故事圖片與音檔及其版本號不用每次重新下載，並只下載有變動的部分

小測驗的答題結果只用來在當下顯示星星，不會被保存。解除安裝本 App（Android 也可在系統設定中「清除資料」），即可一併刪除上述所有本機資料。

3. 意見回饋（只有家長主動寄信時）

  • 設定頁的「意見回饋」會開啟您裝置上的郵件程式，並預先填好收件信箱、App 版本、系統版本與語言設定，方便我們了解問題。
  • 信件是否寄出由您決定；本 App 本身不會傳送任何內容。您寄出後，我們會收到您的電子郵件地址與信件內容，只用來回覆您的問題。

4. 我們「不」蒐集的資料

  • 不蒐集姓名、生日、性別、住址、電話、電子郵件等個人身分資料（您主動寄來的回饋信除外）。
  • 不使用麥克風、相機，不蒐集位置資訊、通訊錄、相簿、行事曆等裝置內容。
  • 不蒐集廣告識別碼（IDFA／AAID），不做使用者追蹤、行為分析或個人化廣告。
  • 本 App 未內建任何廣告、分析（Analytics）或第三方追蹤 SDK。
  • 本 App 沒有社群功能、留言、聊天或使用者間的互動，孩子無法公開分享任何內容。

二、第三方服務

1. Google Firebase（內容下載）

  • Cloud Storage：如第一節第 1 點所述，本 App 只從這裡下載故事內容與版本資訊，不會上傳任何關於您或孩子的資料。
  • 詳細說明請見 Firebase 隱私權說明。

2. Apple App Store 與 Google Play

  • 本 App 經由 App Store 或 Google Play 下載與更新。
  • 「幫我們評分」與「前往更新」會開啟商店上本 App 的頁面，評分與評論由 Apple 或 Google 依其各自的隱私權政策處理，本 App 看不到您填寫的內容。

與所有網路連線一樣，上述服務會收到裝置的 IP 位址以便回應，並可能為了安全與維運目的，短期保留標準的技術紀錄。我們不會用這些資訊辨識、追蹤或分析任何人。除上述服務之外，本 App 不會將任何資料傳送給其他第三方，也不會為了行銷或任何目的出售、出租或交換使用者資料。

三、兒童隱私（COPPA）

本 App 以幼兒為主要使用者，我們依美國《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）的標準處理，並刻意採取「最小化蒐集」的設計：不建立帳號、不做身分辨識、不投放廣告、不做行為追蹤。

  • 我們不向兒童蒐集個人資料。App 從不要求孩子提供姓名或其他資訊，不錄音、不拍照，也沒有聊天、分享或公開發佈的功能。
  • 會離開 App 的功能（評分、意見回饋）以及設定頁，前面都設有家長驗證（一道算術題），孩子無法自行進入。
  • 意見回饋信由家長自行寄出，我們不會要求在信中提供孩子的任何資料。

我們建議由家長或監護人陪同幼兒使用本 App。若您認為我們可能持有孩子的資料，請與我們聯絡（見第八節），我們會查明並儘速處理。

Introduction

KidoBuuk (the “App”) is an audio storybook app for children aged 2 to 6. Children learn about everyday things through story scenes and review them with a short “look at the picture, pick the sound” quiz. The App is developed and operated by CityFun (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the App handles, why, and where it goes.

Summary: The App requires no sign-up and no account, and it does not collect names, birthdays, phone numbers, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.

The App does not use the microphone, camera, or location. It has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no purchases.

The App only downloads story content from Google Firebase; it never uploads anything about you or your child. Your settings stay on your own device.

1. Data We Handle

1.1 Story content downloads

  • The App downloads the story list and covers, story pictures and audio, and a small file describing the latest App version from Google Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • These are downloads only. No account, device identifier, or anything that identifies you or your child is attached to them.

1.2 Data stored on your device (never leaves the device)

The following is kept in the App’s private storage on your device, readable only by the App, and is never uploaded:

Quiz answers are only used to show stars on screen and are not saved. Uninstalling the App (or, on Android, clearing its data in system settings) removes all of the above.

1.3 Feedback (only when a parent chooses to email us)

  • “Send Feedback” in Settings opens the email app on your device with our address, the App version, the OS version, and the language setting filled in, so we can understand the issue.
  • You decide whether to send it; the App itself sends nothing. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply to you.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • No names, birthdays, gender, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses (except in feedback emails you choose to send).
  • No microphone or camera use; no location, contacts, photos, calendars, or other device content.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID); no user tracking, behavioral profiling, or personalized advertising.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The App has no social features, comments, chat, or interaction between users; children cannot publicly share anything.

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Google Firebase (content downloads)

  • Cloud Storage: As described in 1.1, the App only downloads story content and version information from here, and never uploads anything about you or your child.
  • See the Firebase privacy information for details.

2.2 The Apple App Store and Google Play

  • The App is downloaded and updated through the App Store or Google Play.
  • “Rate This App” and “Update” open the App’s store page. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the App cannot see what you write.

As with any network connection, these services receive your device’s IP address in order to respond, and may briefly keep standard technical logs for security and operations. We do not use this information to identify, track, or profile anyone. Apart from these services, the App sends no data to any other third party, and we never sell, rent, or trade user data for marketing or any other purpose.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

The App is designed for young children. We follow the standards of the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and deliberately collect as little as possible: no accounts, no identification, no ads, no behavioral tracking.

  • We do not collect personal information from children. The App never asks a child for a name or anything else, does not record audio or take photos, and has no chat, sharing, or public posting features.
  • Settings and every feature that leaves the App (rating, feedback) are behind a parental gate (a simple math question), so children cannot reach them on their own.
  • Feedback emails are sent by parents, and we never ask for any information about your child in them.

We recommend that a parent or guardian accompany young children when using the App. If you believe we may hold information about your child, please contact us (see Section 8) and we will look into it promptly.

5. Retention and Security

  • We have no user accounts and no database of user data.
  • The on-device data in 1.2 is kept until you uninstall the App. Downloaded content lives in the App’s cache folder; the system may clear it when storage runs low, and it will be downloaded again.
  • Feedback emails are kept only until your question has been handled; you may ask us to delete them at any time.
  • All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Your Rights

Because we keep no data that identifies you or your child, apart from feedback emails you choose to send, we have no personal records to look up, correct, or export. You control the on-device data as follows:

  • Deletion: Uninstall the App, or on Android clear its data under Settings → Apps → KidoBuuk → Storage, to remove all local data.
  • Stop transfers: Turn off network access to stop all downloads; stories already downloaded can still be read.
  • Feedback emails: Email us to access or delete messages you have sent; we will respond within one month.

We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act and other U.S. state privacy laws. If you live in a U.S. state or another region that gives you privacy rights, you may contact us at the address in Section 8. Rights for users in the EEA and the UK are described below.

Children (GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code)

The App’s users are young children. In addition to the practices in Section 3, we designed the App around the GDPR’s special protection of children’s data and the principles of the ICO’s Age Appropriate Design Code:

  • High privacy by default: No account, no identity data, and no options that lower privacy protection.
  • Data minimization: We handle only what is needed to provide the features.
  • No profiling, no tracking: No behavioral analysis, no ads, no location data.
  • No sharing: Data is not shared with anyone beyond the services listed in Section 2 that are needed to provide the features.
  • Parental controls: Settings and every feature that leaves the App are behind a parental gate; the App has no social, chat, or public sharing features.
